## Provenance controls

Machine-readable provenance lives in [`content/manifests/`](content/manifests/). Each record includes a URL, publisher, stated license, retrieval date, SHA-256 content hash, allowed-use class, attribution text, and a normalized excerpt reviewed for candidate generation. In-app source pages repeat the learner-facing attribution.

License labels here report the source terms identified at retrieval; they are not a universal legal conclusion. Reusers are responsible for checking current terms and applicable law in their jurisdiction.
