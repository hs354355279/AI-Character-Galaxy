# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Learners ages 12 to 15 use AI Character Galaxy while studying history and literature. They need to understand who influenced, trusted, opposed, or changed another person without losing the evidence behind each relationship. Teachers and judges may review the experience, but the active learning workflow is designed for an individual learner using a desktop, tablet, or phone.

## Product Purpose

AI Character Galaxy turns reviewed lesson packs into explorable relationship maps. Success means a learner can select a person, understand the structure and meaning of their relationships, inspect supporting evidence, complete guided missions, and finish the lesson without an account, an API key, WebGL, or open-ended chat.

## Brand Personality

Curatorial, spatial, and evidence-led. The product should feel like a contemporary editorial exhibition with the clarity of a serious learning tool. It should create curiosity and a sense of discovery while remaining calm enough for sustained reading and precise interaction.

## Anti-references

- Ordinary SaaS dashboards built from repeated cards, generic glass panels, and decorative metrics.
- Dense admin consoles where every region carries the same visual weight.
- Generic science-fiction interfaces, neon cockpit chrome, spaceship imagery, or spectacle that obscures learning.
- Childish classroom decoration, gamified badges, or cartoon treatment that underestimates learners.
- Portfolio-style motion that delays access to course content or makes evidence difficult to read.

## Design Principles

1. Make relationships spatially legible. Position, hierarchy, and motion must explain the network rather than decorate it.
2. Keep evidence one action away. Selection should reveal the supporting source without breaking exploration flow.
3. Let the learning task lead. Navigation, mission progress, and content hierarchy must remain obvious inside the exhibition language.
4. Preserve calm contrast. Large editorial moments can frame the journey, while active product surfaces stay crisp, readable, and restrained.
5. Provide equivalent paths. The 2D relationship list, keyboard controls, and WebGL fallback must preserve the complete learning outcome.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for text contrast, focus visibility, keyboard operation, and semantic navigation. Respect reduced motion and reduced transparency. Never use color alone to communicate relationship type or progress. Keep a complete 2D alternative to the 3D scene, preserve readable text while zooming, and support mobile layouts without horizontal document overflow.
