# OpenAI Build Week Demo Script

Target length: **02:45**. Public submission video must be under three minutes and include voiceover. Record at 16:9; use edited cuts between verified application states rather than rushing live interaction.

## 00:00–00:15 · The learner problem

**Visual:** Let the loading curtain reveal the orbital exhibition hero, then open the course register.

**Voiceover:** “Students often memorize names and plot points without seeing the relationships that made events change. AI Character Galaxy turns those connections into an evidence-grounded map learners can explore.”

## 00:15–00:33 · Product promise

**Visual:** Use the persistent top navigation to open the French Revolution introduction; pause on the generated observatory artwork, essential question, and objectives.

**Voiceover:** “This Build Week Education project includes two complete lessons for ages twelve to fifteen. There is no account, no open-ended chat, and no required API key. Each official connection links back to reviewed evidence.”

## 00:33–01:02 · French Revolution galaxy

**Visual:** Enter the observatory, show the shader planets and particle field, select Rousseau and Robespierre from the stable people index, then switch to 2D and open their influence relationship.

**Voiceover:** “The 3D galaxy makes groups and influence spatial, while the synchronized 2D list preserves the full lesson for keyboard users and devices without WebGL. Here Rousseau’s ideas connect to Robespierre, and the evidence panel explains exactly what the source supports.”

## 01:02–01:25 · GPT-5.6 enhancement

**Visual:** Click **Explain with GPT-5.6**; show the validated or reviewed-fallback label and evidence IDs. Cut to **Characters**, search the reviewed atlas, then query Mary Wollstonecraft and show the generated label with citations.

**Voiceover:** “GPT-5.6 adds an age-appropriate lesson explanation through the Responses API and Zod Structured Outputs. A separate web-grounded research surface can investigate another named character, always labeled as generated and displayed with citations. Reviewed lessons never depend on it.”

## 01:25–01:43 · Missions and provenance

**Visual:** Show mission-complete feedback, the Rousseau-to-Louis path, then the source page.

**Voiceover:** “Mission completion is deterministic and local; the model never grades the learner. Source attribution, license notes, evidence summaries, disputed interpretations, and content hashes stay visible and reviewable.”

## 01:43–02:01 · Romeo and Juliet transfer

**Visual:** Cut to Romeo and Juliet. Select Romeo, Friar Laurence, and Juliet; show an act-and-scene locator and the comparison response.

**Voiceover:** “The same interaction model transfers to literature. Learners trace the fragile plan through Friar Laurence, compare Romeo and Juliet’s choices, and explain how relationships create a turning point. Literary evidence includes act and scene locators.”

## 02:01–02:17 · Complete learning loop

**Visual:** Prepared assessment followed by **My Relationship Discoveries**.

**Voiceover:** “After five missions, learners answer a five-prompt comprehension check and receive a private discoveries summary. Progress stays only in this browser session and resumes after a refresh or source detour.”

## 02:17–02:33 · Reproducible content pipeline

**Visual:** Terminal running `npm run content:validate` and `npm run content:review`; briefly show both zero-blocker reports.

**Voiceover:** “GPT-5.6 also powers a candidate-only generation pipeline. Reviewed manifests fix source permissions and excerpts. Generated packs cannot overwrite official lessons and need schema checks plus human editorial approval.”

## 02:33–02:45 · Codex and close

**Visual:** Test summary, production build routes, then return to the galaxy hero.

**Voiceover:** “Codex accelerated the architecture, image direction, implementation, test-first milestones, and browser debugging. Automated checks and twelve real-browser journeys keep the observatory evidence-first, accessible, and ready to extend. AI Character Galaxy: every person has a universe of relationships.”

## Recording checklist

- Use English voiceover; captions recommended.
- Keep the final public YouTube video under 03:00.
- Show both the reviewed-content GPT-5.6 label and the separately labeled web-grounded character research result.
- Show both lessons, source evidence, the content pipeline, Codex contribution, and the final summary.
